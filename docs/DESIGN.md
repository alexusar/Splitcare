# Family Matters visual and UX direction — proposal

## Brand
**Family Matters** with tagline “Your people. Your plans. One place.” SplitCare becomes the **Expenses → AppleCare One** module, not the whole product identity.

## Visual language
- Deep midnight navy / charcoal surfaces; lilac accents; mint for verified, amber for pending, pink for actions requiring attention.
- Soft ambient gradients and animated orbs with reduced-motion support.
- Rounded cards, airy spacing, carefully labeled buttons, mobile-first navigation.
- Never rely on color alone: status text, icons, and accessible labels.
- Friendly, trustworthy finance presentation; do not gamify actual money movement or imply screenshot = settlement.

## Page-by-page mockup specification
1. **Home** — greeting, family cards, due soon, current AppleCare status, chores, next trip, activity.
2. **Expenses** — family expense groups, amount due, statuses, add expense, totals and recent activity.
3. **AppleCare** — monthly view, per-member approval statuses, spin wheel, weighted outcomes, credit wallet, Zelle handoff, private proof.
4. **Chores** — lists, filtering, assignee, recurrence, optional point scores, due-dates and approvals.
5. **Trips & Calendar** — proposed trips, free/busy dates, member availability, date votes, optional itinerary.
6. **Family Admin** — invite member, invitation status, roles, permissions, notification/privacy settings.

Interactive wireframes live at [docs/mockups/](mockups/index.html) and will appear on GitHub Pages at `/Splitcare/docs/mockups/`. **Mockups are not production features.** A six-screen visual concept was also generated during planning but has not been committed as a binary asset.

## Responsive principles
- Desktop uses left sidebar or top navigation plus two-column content.
- Mobile uses compact navigation, touch targets ≥44px, single column and horizontal-overflow-safe tables.
- Empty states, loading states, error states, private-data caution notes; keyboard focus visible.
- Mobile website should be excellent before considering native apps.
