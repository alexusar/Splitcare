# Domain glossary

- **Family:** a private group of related accounts sharing organizational data.
- **Admin:** authorized member allowed to manage invitations, rules, payment approvals and resets.
- **Member:** non-admin account, scoped to its own records and permitted shared records.
- **Expense Group:** ongoing financial purpose, e.g. AppleCare One or trip lodging.
- **Cycle:** billing period for one expense group, identified by calendar month/year and timezone.
- **Base Contribution:** member's normal amount owed before wheel or credits.
- **Spin:** immutable probabilistic determination of a contribution multiplier within one cycle.
- **Waiver:** zero charge caused by a free spin; not evidence of funds received.
- **Payment Submission:** assertion/proof of transfer, awaiting independent review.
- **Payment Confirmation:** admin decision that money was received.
- **Credit:** previously confirmed excess payment, usable against eligible future charges.
- **Ledger:** append-only source of truth for credit earned, applied and reversed.
- **Chore Template:** task definition with optional recurrence.
- **Chore Occurrence:** one due instance; completed independently from future occurrences.
- **Trip Proposal:** destination/date/budget suggestion.
- **Availability Vote:** private free/busy answer for a proposed date; not a synced calendar event.
- **Family Activity:** permission-scoped log of meaningful actions.
- **Receipt OCR:** extraction of apparent text values, not transfer settlement verification.
